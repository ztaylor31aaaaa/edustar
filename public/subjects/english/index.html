English

Improve your reading, writing, vocabulary and ability to explain ideas clearly.

Building a strong paragraph

A clear paragraph usually focuses on one main idea. Start with a topic sentence, add evidence or examples, explain how they support the idea, then link back to the question.

Simple structure: Point → Evidence → Explanation → Link. This is often called PEEL.

Sentence variety

Short sentences can make an important point stand out. Longer sentences can connect related ideas. Use both, and check that every sentence is clear and correctly punctuated.

Try it

  1. Write a topic sentence about why exercise is useful.
  2. Add one example that supports it.
  3. Explain how your example proves the point.
