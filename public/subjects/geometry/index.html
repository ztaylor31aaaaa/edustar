Geometry

Learn about angles, shapes, measurement, area and the properties of 2D figures.

Types of angles

  • Acute: less than 90°.
  • Right: exactly 90°.
  • Obtuse: more than 90° but less than 180°.
  • Straight: exactly 180°.
  • Reflex: more than 180° but less than 360°.

Perimeter and area

Perimeter is the distance around the outside of a 2D shape. Area is the amount of surface inside it.

Rectangle example: length 8 cm and width 3 cm. Perimeter = 2 × (8 + 3) = 22 cm. Area = 8 × 3 = 24 cm².

Try it

  1. What type of angle is 45°?
  2. Find the perimeter of a 5 cm by 4 cm rectangle.
  3. Find its area.
Show answers

1. Acute. 2. 18 cm. 3. 20 cm².
