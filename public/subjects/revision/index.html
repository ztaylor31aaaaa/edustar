STUDENT LEARNING · YEAR 7

Revision

Use short practice questions to review important skills from different subjects.

Mathematics check

  1. Convert 3/4 to a percentage.
  2. Simplify 7x + 2x.
  3. Find the area of a rectangle that is 6 cm long and 4 cm wide.
Show answers

1. 75%. 2. 9x. 3. 24 cm².

Science check

  1. Name the three common states of matter.
  2. What is a producer in a food chain?
Show answers

1. Solid, liquid and gas. 2. An organism, usually a plant, that makes its own food.

English check

Write a short paragraph explaining why teamwork can be useful. Include a clear point, an example and an explanation.
