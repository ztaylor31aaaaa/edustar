Fractions, decimals and percentages

These are three ways to describe parts of a whole. A fraction shows a part out of an equal number of parts, a decimal uses place value, and a percentage means “out of 100”.

Example: 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%. They all represent one half.

To convert a fraction to a percentage, first write it as a decimal, then multiply by 100.

Integers

Integers are whole numbers, including negative numbers, zero and positive numbers. On a number line, values increase as you move to the right.

Example: −3 + 5 = 2. Starting at −3 and moving five places right lands on 2.

Try it

  1. Write 0.25 as a percentage.
  2. Which is larger: −2 or −6?
  3. Find 10% of 80.
