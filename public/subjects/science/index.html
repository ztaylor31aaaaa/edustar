Science

Explore how living things, materials and forces interact with the world around us.

Living things and ecosystems

An ecosystem includes living organisms and the non-living parts of their environment. Organisms depend on each other for food, shelter and other resources.

Food chain: grass → grasshopper → frog → snake. The arrows show the direction energy moves as organisms eat one another.

States of matter

Most materials around us can be described as solids, liquids or gases. In a solid, particles stay in fixed positions and vibrate. In a liquid, particles can move past each other. In a gas, particles move freely and spread out.
